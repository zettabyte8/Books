## A note on this collaboration

This book was made by three parties. Thomas G. Coleman designed the inquiry and wrote every prompt. Gemini produced the first draft across a single 2026 session of fifteen prompts. Venice AI then audited that draft, identified thirteen classes of defect — missing pedagogy, an unusable reading level, absent case studies, four contested findings stated as settled — and rebuilt it into the text you hold.

Nothing here is hidden. Appendix C reproduces the original prompt sequence in full, annotated, so a student can see exactly how the argument was assembled and where a machine was guessing. Where the audit found the first draft wrong, this edition says so in the open, in a **Contested** box. That transparency is not decoration. It is the book's own thesis applied to the book.

Readers should treat every citation in Appendix D as a lead to verify, not as a guarantee.

## How to use this book

Each chapter runs the same sequence: **objectives, key terms, text, primary source, contested findings where relevant, comprehension checks, one activity, summary.** The apparatus is not padding. Reading objectives before the text roughly doubles retention; the checks test whether you actually followed the argument; the activities move you from knowing to doing.

**Three reading routes exist.**

- **The short course (six sessions):** Chapters 1, 2, 3, 9, 10, 11.
- **The full course (a term):** all twelve, in order, with Appendix E.
- **The reference route:** Chapters 1–3 as a toolkit, then jump to whichever case study you need.

**Symbols.** 🔧 Activity. ⚠️ Contested. 📄 Primary source. → Cross-reference.

## Note to educators

Every chapter carries objectives written to be copyable into a lesson plan. Appendix E holds a full scheme of work, answer key, and assessment rubrics. The book is deliberately structured so a class can run Chapter 10 (defence) in week two and use it against Chapters 6–9 for the rest of the term.

Sensitive material appears in Chapter 6 (atrocity propaganda, genocide) and Chapter 8 (Rwanda). Both are handled without assuming prior knowledge.

## Conventions

**Light and dark** are analytic poles, not moral verdicts. Plenty of campaigns are both.

**"Mass"** means a population large enough that no individual can verify the claims being made about it — roughly, more people than you can know personally. The threshold is practical, not numerical.

**Contested boxes** mark findings where competent researchers disagree. They are the most important pages in the book. A text that never says "we don't know" is teaching you to trust it too much.
