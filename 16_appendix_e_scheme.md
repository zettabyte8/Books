## Answer key (sample)

**Ch 3 q2.** Laugh track = social proof, because the audience is uncertain how funny the material is and uses the crowd's reaction as evidence.

**Ch 8 q2.** Shared sacrifice, visible fairness, continuous feedback, stated reason.

**Ch 11 q2.** Pre-ticked box fails Truthfulness (omits the choice), Authenticity (hides that it is a choice), Respect (assumes inertia), Equity (targets the inattentive), and Social responsibility (aggregate over-collection).

**Ch 12 q1.** The dividend works because doubt is cheaper than verification; a plausible forgery is enough to make "prove it's real" a live question.

## Rubric — case analysis (20 marks)

- **4** correct use of chapter terminology without strain
- **4** at least two sources cited with dates
- **4** one Contested box engaged, not avoided
- **4** counter-argument steel-manned
- **4** conclusion calibrated, not absolute

## Rubric — activity output (10 marks)

- **3** instructions followed fully
- **3** evidence recorded, not asserted
- **2** reflection names a limitation
- **2** clear to a stranger
