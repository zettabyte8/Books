# Appendix A: Glossary

**Agnotology** — the deliberate production and maintenance of public ignorance or doubt. (Proctor)

**Anchoring** — over-reliance on the first number encountered.

**Arousal audit** — a timed pause to name the emotion a message is inducing before acting.

**Astroturfing** — a centrally funded campaign disguised as spontaneous grassroots activity.

**Availability heuristic** — judging probability by how easily examples come to mind.

**Blowback** — the discrediting of a campaign by the campaign's own visibility.

**Boosting** — building a person's competence rather than steering their choice; the main alternative to nudging.

**Cadena** — a mandatory simultaneous broadcast, pre-empting all other channels. (Venezuela)

**Carriage control** — control over distribution slots (frequency, cable package, platform) rather than over content or disclosure.

**Choice architecture** — the designed shape of a set of options. Unavoidable.

**Clientelism** — a reciprocal arrangement between office-holder and broadcaster in which favourable coverage is traded for licences or revenue.

**Cognitive dissonance** — discomfort from holding contradictory beliefs, resolved by adjusting belief to fit action.

**Cognitive liberty** — the right to self-determination over one's own mental states.

**Dark pattern** — an interface designed to produce a choice the user did not intend.

**Deindividuation** — loss of self-awareness in a group, with reduced inhibition.

**Editorial interference** — direction from owners or officials to journalists about coverage, whether written or customary.

**Epistemic flooding** — overwhelming a public with volume and contradiction to induce exhaustion.

**Filter bubble** — algorithmic narrowing of exposure to the similar. (Pariser)

**Forwarding economy** — a distribution model in which reach comes from users resending messages rather than from a broadcaster.

**Illusory truth effect** — repetition increases believability independently of truth or source.

**Inoculation / prebunking** — exposure to a weakened manipulation technique plus its name, to build resistance.

**Integration propaganda** — continuous, ambient propaganda that adapts a population to its institutions. (Ellul)

**Lateral reading** — leaving a page to check the publisher against independent sources.

**Liar's dividend** — the advantage gained from being able to call authentic evidence fake.

**Loss aversion** — losses weigh roughly twice equivalent gains.

**Manufactured consent** — consent produced by managed information rather than genuine agreement.

**Microtargeting** — tailoring messages to narrow inferred audience segments.

**Must-carry** — a rule obliging a distributor to carry a given channel, or a channel to carry a given feed.

**Neuroright** — a legal protection over neural data or mental process.

**Overton window** — the band of policies currently considered acceptable; shifting it is a distinct goal from winning an argument. (Overton, c. 1995 — a heuristic, not a measurement)

**Panopticon** — Bentham's prison design in which possible observation produces self-discipline.

**Platform-party machinery** — party-run distribution across messaging platforms, bypassing broadcast and regulator alike.

**Prebunking** — see inoculation.

**Propaganda model** — Herman and Chomsky's five structural filters on news.

**Pseudo-environment** — the simplified mental picture of the world built by media. (Lippmann)

**Public broadcaster capture** — the conversion of a public broadcaster into a compliant one through appointments, budget, and licence pressure.

**Publicity principle** — a tactic is honest if its designer would defend the mechanism in public to those steered.

**Second-order effect** — a consequence of a campaign's success rather than of its failure.

**SIFT** — Stop, Investigate, Find better coverage, Trace. (Caulfield)

**Social proof** — copying the majority under uncertainty.

**Spiral of silence** — suppression of minority views as perceived support falls. (Noelle-Neumann)

**State monopoly broadcaster** — a single state-owned channel with no licensed competitor.

**Steel-manning** — arguing the strongest form of the opposing case before rebutting it.

**TARES** — Truthfulness, Authenticity, Respect, Equity, Social responsibility. (Baker & Martinson)

**Unity** — compliance driven by shared identity rather than similarity. (Cialdini, 2016)

**Zero-rating** — exempting an app from data charges, which favours it over competitors and shapes where users go.
