## Full-term route

## Answer key (sample)

**Ch 3 q2.** Laugh track = social proof, because the audience is uncertain how funny the material is and uses the crowd's reaction as evidence.

**Ch 3 q4.** Exhausting an opponent changes what is *speakable* and survives a change of government; refuting them wins one decision and leaves the window where it was.

**Ch 4 q3.** Triangulate, almost entirely — the centre was redefined so the policy sat outside it. The carbon case also used exhaust: cap-and-trade became boring as well as tribal.

**Ch 6 q3.** Speer wrote a bestselling memoir after twenty years' imprisonment, exculpating himself and inflating his improvisational genius; historians (Sereny, Evans) dismantled the account. Use Welch for the apparatus, photographs for the effect, and the memoir only as testimony.

**Ch 8 q2.** Shared sacrifice, visible fairness, continuous feedback, stated reason.

**Ch 9 q2.** A cadena uses institutional architecture (a decree) to seize interface and information architecture at once, and skips built architecture entirely — the crowd need not be assembled.

**Ch 10 q4.** Interface and information architecture, both seized through forwarding; institutional architecture is nearly absent.

**Ch 11 q1.** Because the interference was written down. A written instruction is evidence; a customary one is not.

**Ch 12 q1.** The near-simultaneous arrival of all four registers within one generation, so the sequence of print, broadcast, platform, and regulation never occurs.

**Ch 12 q3.** That it will regulate platforms using existing broadcast or telecoms powers rather than new platform-specific law — as Nigeria did in 2021.

**Ch 13 q2.** Pre-ticked box fails Truthfulness (omits the choice), Authenticity (hides that it is a choice), Respect (assumes inertia), Equity (targets the inattentive), and Social responsibility (aggregate over-collection).

**Ch 16 q1.** The dividend works because doubt is cheaper than verification; a plausible forgery is enough to make "prove it's real" a live question.

## Rubric — case analysis (20 marks)

- **4** correct use of chapter terminology without strain
- **4** at least two sources cited with dates
- **4** one Contested box engaged, not avoided
- **4** counter-argument steel-manned
- **4** conclusion calibrated, not absolute

## Rubric — activity output (10 marks)

- **3** instructions followed fully
- **3** evidence recorded, not asserted
- **2** reflection names a limitation
- **2** clear to a stranger

## Rubric — thesis-testing report, Ch 12 (10 marks)

- **3** counter-example genuinely located, not invented
- **3** prediction stated in falsifiable form
- **2** verdict given, either way, with reasoning
- **2** Contested box used rather than dodged
